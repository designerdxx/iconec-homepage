Choose

Pick a piece in the app and choose a delivery slot.

A courier hands your pieces over in person, and they’re yours to wear from the first evening.

Wear

It comes to your door, and it is yours to wear for the next 30 days.

A month is long enough for a piece to become part of how you dress. It goes to the office, to dinner and through an ordinary Tuesday.

Swap

From day 2, swap whenever you like.

Choose the next piece and a slot, and we deliver it and collect the one you are wearing.

Return

Each piece goes back by day 30, unless you extend it another 30 days in the app. The courier collects it at your slot.

Still attached on day 30? Keep it on for another 30. The option appears in your last week, so nothing goes back before you are done with it.

Wear the real thing

Real diamond jewellery on a monthly membership. Wear a piece for 30 days, then swap it for the next.

Most diamonds spend their lives waiting in a drawer.

You buy one piece, once. It costs what a holiday costs, so it comes out for weddings and goes back in its box. The rest of the year you wear the same three things, and the piece you love most is the one you see least.

One piece.
Thirty days of reasons.

No promises. This is what tends to happen once it is on.

  • A chanceTo wear diamonds on a Tuesday.01

    No occasion needed. It is on you at the school run, in the meeting and at the supermarket till.

  • A chanceTo say yes to the bold one.02

    The piece you would never buy because you would never wear it enough. For a month, enough is not the question.

  • A chanceTo be asked where it is from.03

    And to have a different answer next month.

  • A chanceTo find out what suits you.04

    Try the collar, the drops, the knot ring. Three months of wearing teaches you more than years of looking through a window.

  • A chanceTo send it back without regret.05

    It goes on to its next month. So do you.
